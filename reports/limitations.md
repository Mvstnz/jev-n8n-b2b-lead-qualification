Full-set route quality and three-run stability remain unmeasured. The key has a USD 1 non-resetting budget within USD 5 free credits; no payment was made and sampled actual costs were USD 0. 5. An approved private n8n sender delivered five synthetic MOCK lead notifications and one MOCK summary in one run. The six texts were verified by reading the Slack demo channel. The sender was relocked and remains inactive. These messages are hand-authored policy demonstrations, separate from the five live JEV intake runs; no live intake sent Slack.

The two serial replay tests skipped JEV and append. A Sheets lookup followed by append is not atomic; concurrent duplicate prevention, API key/payload conflict handling, lost-response recovery and unknown Slack delivery have not been proven. Keep the workflow inactive until these controls and credential migration are complete.
